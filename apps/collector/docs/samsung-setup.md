# Samsung setup

Samsung phones stop background apps to save battery. Do these steps once so Collector keeps working.

1. Open Settings > Apps > Collector > Battery. Choose **Unrestricted**.
2. Open Settings > Battery > Background usage limits > **Never auto sleeping apps**. Add Collector. Make sure Collector is not under "Sleeping apps" or "Deep sleeping apps".
3. Open Settings > Apps > Collector > Permissions > Location. Choose **Allow all the time**. Leave "Use precise location" **off**.
4. Open Settings > Apps > Collector > Permissions > Photos and videos. Choose **Allow all**.
5. Open Settings > Apps > Collector. Turn **off** "Pause app activity if unused".
6. In this app, tap "Choose Daylio folder". Then, in Daylio, export a CSV into that folder.
